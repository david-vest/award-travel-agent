Amex Membership Rewards offers a broad airline-partner roster, including
ANA, Aeroplan, Avianca LifeMiles, British Airways, Cathay Pacific, Delta,
Emirates, Flying Blue, Iberia, JetBlue, Qantas, Qatar Airways, Singapore
KrisFlyer, and Virgin Atlantic. United and American are not direct partners.
Check Amex's live transfer screen before relying on this list: partners have
changed recently. Hawaiian transfers ended June 30, 2025, and Etihad
transfers ended June 30, 2026.

Transfer ratios are partner-specific, not universally 1:1. In particular,
Emirates transfers at 5:4, while Cathay Pacific and JetBlue transfer at 1:0.8.
Transfers are irreversible and can take time to post, so confirm an award is
actually bookable before transferring. Promotional transfer bonuses can
improve the arithmetic but should not substitute for confirming availability.

Membership Rewards points are earned across a wide range of Amex cards but
require a card with actual transfer eligibility — the everyday-spending
Blue Cash cards, for instance, earn a different, non-transferable currency,
so the underlying card matters as much as the points balance.
