Bilt transfers 1:1 to United and Atmos Rewards (the combined Alaska/Hawaiian
program), plus Aeroplan, Avianca LifeMiles, Flying Blue, Turkish, Cathay,
Emirates, Etihad, Virgin Atlantic, British Airways, Iberia, TAP, Southwest,
Japan Airlines, Qatar Airways, and others. American Airlines is *not* a
current Bilt transfer partner. The partner list has expanded quickly, so use
Bilt's support page rather than a static list when choosing a program.

Bilt points can be earned through housing payments and other Bilt programs;
the applicable card and transaction requirements determine whether points are
earned. Do not assume unlimited rent earning or a recurring Rent Day bonus.
Those features and promotions are governed by current Bilt terms and can
change. As with every transferable currency, verify award availability before
making an irreversible transfer.
