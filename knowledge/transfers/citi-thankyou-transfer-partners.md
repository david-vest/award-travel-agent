Citi ThankYou transfers to American Airlines AAdvantage as well as a broad
mix of international airline and hotel programs, including Turkish,
LifeMiles, Flying Blue, Cathay Pacific, EVA Air, Qatar Airways, Singapore
KrisFlyer, Virgin Atlantic, JetBlue, Emirates, Etihad, Qantas, and Thai
Airways. Citi still does not directly transfer to United, Delta, or Atmos
Rewards.

Turkish and LifeMiles together are the standout pairing here — both price
Star Alliance business class well and neither adds carrier-imposed fuel
surcharges, so between the two of them Citi points can reach most
transatlantic and transpacific Star Alliance business-class redemptions
without needing a second bank's currency at all.

Eligibility, ratio, and transfer speed depend on the card and partner. Do
not rely on a generic "within a day" promise or assume a Double Cash account
alone has the same transfer options as a premium ThankYou account; verify the
current ThankYou transfer screen before moving points.
