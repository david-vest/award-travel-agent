Chase Ultimate Rewards transfers to Aer Lingus AerClub, Air Canada Aeroplan,
Air France-KLM Flying Blue, British Airways Club, Iberia Club, JetBlue,
Singapore KrisFlyer, Southwest, United, and Virgin Atlantic, plus hotel
partners. Transfers are irreversible; Chase says many post by the next
business day but may take up to seven business days. Confirm live award
availability before transferring.

The list's shape matters as much as its length. Chase covers United
directly, which is useful for a Chicago-based traveler since ORD is a United
hub, and it reaches most of Star Alliance's best sweet spots through
Aeroplan without needing a second bank's points. It has no SkyTeam partner
besides Flying Blue, and no Delta, American, Alaska, ANA, or Cathay Pacific —
those require Amex, Capital One, Citi, or Bilt instead.

Sapphire Preferred, Sapphire Reserve, and Ink Business Preferred provide
transfer access. Points from a no-annual-fee Freedom account can be combined
with an eligible account first. Treat transfer bonuses as time-limited offers,
not an assumed 20–30% rate.
