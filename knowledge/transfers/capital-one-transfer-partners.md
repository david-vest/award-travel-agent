Capital One's airline partners include Aeromexico, Aeroplan, Avianca
LifeMiles, British Airways, Cathay Pacific, Etihad, Finnair, Flying Blue,
Qantas, Qatar Airways, Singapore KrisFlyer, TAP, Turkish Miles&Smiles, and
Virgin Red, as well as Emirates, EVA Air, Japan Airlines, and JetBlue at
reduced ratios. It also has hotel partners. The current list is broader than
this entry's former list, but does not include United, Delta, American, ANA,
or Atmos Rewards.

Most, but not all, airline transfers are 1:1. Emirates, EVA Air, and Japan
Airlines are 2:1.5; JetBlue is 5:3. Check the live Capital One transfer page
for both availability and ratio before doing the award calculation.

The one real friction point is search: Capital One's transfer portal shows a
partner's estimated award cost but doesn't reliably confirm live seat
availability the way a dedicated award search tool does, so miles can be
transferred to a partner before confirming the seat is actually there.
Because transfers are one-way, it's worth verifying availability on the
partner's own site or a search tool first, then transferring only once a
specific bookable flight is identified.
