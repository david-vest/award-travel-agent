United MileagePlus' Excursionist Perk lets an award itinerary that visits
three or more regions add one extra one-way flight for free, as long as that
segment stays within a region already being visited rather than crossing an
ocean a second time. A Chicago-Tokyo-Bangkok-Chicago itinerary, for example,
prices as if it were only Chicago-Tokyo-Chicago — the Tokyo-Bangkok leg,
entirely within Asia, does not add mileage cost.

This effectively makes a third flight free on the right itinerary shape, and
it stacks with United's general allowance of a single stopover on a
round-trip award, so a well-built itinerary can include a stopover in one
region plus a free Excursionist segment in another for the price of a single
round-trip.

The catch is that it only works as a single continuous multi-city award
booked all at once — it cannot be added to an existing simple round trip
after the fact — and United's website multi-city tool does not reliably
apply the perk correctly, so building the itinerary by phone with an agent
who understands the rule is often the more reliable path. Partner saver
space must also be available on every segment simultaneously, and adding a
third region to the search makes that meaningfully harder to find than a
standard round trip.
