Alaska Mileage Plan prices Japan Airlines business class (Sky Suite) on the
Chicago-Haneda nonstop at roughly 60,000-70,000 miles one-way, noticeably
below what JAL's own Mileage Bank chart charges outside travelers, and with
no fuel surcharges added. First class, when it is released, prices higher
but still compares well against booking the same seat through most other
currencies.

The Chicago routing is a real advantage here — JAL operates ORD-HND
nonstop, so this is a one-flight redemption rather than a connection through
Tokyo Narita or a West Coast gateway, which matters for a nine-plus-hour
sector.

The catches are availability and website reliability, both familiar themes
across JAL and Alaska partner bookings. JAL releases only one or two award
seats per flight even in economy-heavy widebody configurations, and Alaska's
website frequently fails to display JAL space — including first class — that
does exist and is bookable by phone. Building a plan around a specific date
rather than a flexible window makes this materially harder, since a single
sold-out flight forces a full re-search rather than a small adjustment.
Alaska's own co-branded card and Bilt are the practical ways to accumulate
Mileage Plan miles for this kind of redemption.
