Avianca LifeMiles commonly does not add carrier-imposed fuel surcharges to
Star Alliance partner awards, which can make Lufthansa Group redemptions much
cheaper in cash than programs that pass them through. Mileage pricing is not
safely represented by a single Chicago-to-Europe number: it depends on the
current chart, route, mixed-cabin treatment, and booking flow. Price the
itinerary before transferring.

LifeMiles uses its own distance-based chart, similar in spirit to Aeroplan's,
so the exact price shifts with routing rather than being a single flat
number for "US to Europe." It is a genuinely useful complement to Turkish
Miles&Smiles for the same trip type — where Miles&Smiles requires flying a
partner rather than Turkish's own metal, LifeMiles works cleanly on
Lufthansa Group carriers that Miles&Smiles prices differently.

The catch is program stability. LifeMiles has changed its award chart and
pricing with limited advance notice more than once, so a saved search from
a year ago is not a reliable guide to today's price. Miles can expire after
account inactivity. Consult current LifeMiles terms for any close-in or
service fee rather than asserting a universal fee. Bilt, Capital One,
Citi, and Amex transfer to LifeMiles subject to their current transfer terms.
