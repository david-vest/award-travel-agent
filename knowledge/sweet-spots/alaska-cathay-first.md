Cathay Pacific's A350-1000 does not have a first-class cabin. Cathay First is
offered on selected Boeing 777-300ER aircraft, so an A350 itinerary cannot
be a Cathay First redemption. Alaska's award chart prices a US-Asia Cathay
First one-way at roughly 70,000 miles — high five figures, well inside what
Cathay's own Asia Miles chart or most other partner currencies would ask for
the same seat. Confirm both the operating aircraft and cabin, and check the
current chart for any recent changes, before transferring or buying miles.

Alaska also permits a free one-way stopover on partner awards, so a first
class redemption to Hong Kong can be extended into a second Asia stop at no
extra mileage cost — a meaningful value-add on a redemption that is already
priced well.

Cathay First partner availability is exceptionally limited. Alaska now uses
the Atmos Rewards program; Bilt transfers to Atmos at 1:1. Confirm the exact
award online or with Alaska before a transfer, rather than assuming a phone
agent can access inventory the website cannot.
