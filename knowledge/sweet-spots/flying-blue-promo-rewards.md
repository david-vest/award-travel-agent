Flying Blue publishes a monthly Promo Rewards calendar — a published list of
specific routes and travel windows priced well below the standard chart,
sometimes 25-50% off. It is unusual among major programs for discounting at
the route-and-date level rather than only through opaque dynamic pricing, so
a traveler who checks the calendar can find real discounts rather than
guessing.

Business class Chicago to Paris or Amsterdam that normally runs over 100,000
miles round-trip on the standard chart has appeared on Promo Rewards closer
to 50,000-55,000 miles round-trip for the discounted dates. The discount
applies to the mileage price only, and saver-level seats in the promotional
inventory are genuinely limited, so a route appearing on the calendar is not
a guarantee of finding an open seat on a specific date.

The catch is timing and surcharges. Promo Rewards windows are typically
announced only a few weeks before the travel dates they cover, which does
not suit travelers who need to lock in flights far in advance. Flying Blue
also passes along carrier-imposed surcharges on some Air France and KLM
awards, so the discounted mileage price is not the full picture — check the
taxes-and-fees total at checkout. Chase, Amex, Capital One, Citi, and Bilt
all transfer to Flying Blue at 1:1.
