Virgin Atlantic can redeem points for ANA awards, but the former 120,000-point
round-trip First-Class figure is obsolete. Virgin prices ANA awards by route
distance and cabin; check the live partner chart or agent quote for the exact
one-way price before transferring points.

The routing works well from Chicago: ANA flies ORD-HND/NRT nonstop, so no
connection is needed to reach the first-class cabin. Surcharges are a real
cost here, not a footnote: Virgin Atlantic Flying Club generally passes
through ANA's full carrier-imposed fuel surcharge, which commonly runs
several hundred dollars each way and can exceed a thousand on some
itineraries — price the full cash component before judging the redemption.

The catch is availability, not price. ANA typically releases only a single
first-class award seat per flight, sometimes none at all on popular dates,
and Virgin Atlantic's website does not reliably display ANA first-class
space even when it exists — booking almost always requires a phone call to
Flying Club. Chase, Amex, Capital One, Citi, and Bilt all transfer to Virgin
Atlantic at 1:1, so building a balance ahead of a target date is
straightforward; finding the seat to redeem it on is the harder part.
