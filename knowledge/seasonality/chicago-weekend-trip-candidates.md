A genuine weekend award trip from Chicago — out Thursday or Friday night,
back Sunday night or early Monday — is realistically limited to
destinations reachable in well under nine hours each way, since anything
longer eats too far into the trip itself on a two-night turnaround. That
rules out Asia entirely from a practical weekend-trip standpoint, but it
leaves a useful set of transatlantic and domestic options.

Reykjavik and Dublin are the standout transatlantic candidates from
Chicago, both roughly seven-and-a-half to eight hours nonstop, with
favorable time zones that put an overnight outbound flight landing Friday or
Saturday morning local time, leaving a full weekend before a Sunday-night
return. This only works comfortably in a premium cabin with a lie-flat seat,
since there's no recovery day on either end — a long-haul red-eye in
economy on both legs of a compressed weekend is genuinely rough.

Domestically, saver-level awards from ORD can make a weekend trip affordable,
but price and availability vary by date and program. Miami is not a United
hub, and Avios is not a Star Alliance currency: it may price eligible
Oneworld or other partner flights, whereas Aeroplan can price eligible Star
Alliance flights. Weekend trips depend more on
finding saver space on a specific Friday and Sunday than on the destination
itself, since weekend dates are exactly when partner space is tightest.
